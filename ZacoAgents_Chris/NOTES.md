# Notes

Fill this in as you go. Two pages at most. It is read closely.

## What I built

How to run it, and what it does.

## Calls I made, and why

Anywhere the requirements left a choice to you, or where you had to decide what
the system should do because the documents did not answer the question.

Where you picked a threshold, a weight or a banding, say what you picked and
what the reasoning was.

## What I found in the data

Anything the requirements do not mention. What you did about it.

## What I did not do

What you left out, and why. What you would do next, and what it would change.

## What I am unsure of

Figures you would not settle money against yet, and what would have to be true
for you to trust them.
